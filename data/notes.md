[N1] Closing rank is the rank of the last candidate allotted a seat in the final round. A candidate with a rank equal to or better (lower number) than the closing rank would have received that seat in that year.

[N2] Past closing ranks are not predictions. Ranks move every year with the number of candidates, seats added and changes in reservation policy.

[N3] Reserved-category seats (OBC, SC, ST, EWS) use the candidate's category rank, not the all-India rank. General seats use the all-India rank.

[N4] State quota seats in Government colleges are normally open only to candidates domiciled in that state. All-India quota seats are open to all candidates.

[N5] Private colleges charge tuition fees set by the college or state committee. This dataset does not contain fee information.

[N6] This dataset does not contain hostel, stipend, bond or placement information. Questions about these are outside the sources.

[N7] Data covers the years 2023, 2024 and 2025, final round only. There is no data for any other year.

[N8] All colleges, ranks and seat counts in this dataset are synthetic and invented for a technical assignment. They do not describe any real institution.
